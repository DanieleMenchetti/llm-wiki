# Complex Carbohydrates

## Definition
Carbohydrates composed of longer sugar molecule chains that digest more slowly, providing sustained energy release and stable blood sugar levels.

## Key Aspects
- Release energy slowly and gradually
- Maintain stable blood sugar levels
- Prevent concentration drops typical of rapid sugars
- Provide constant fuel to brain and muscles
- Found in whole grains (brown rice, oats, quinoa, buckwheat), legumes, potatoes
- Rich in B vitamins important for energy metabolism
- Brown rice also contains vitamin E (antioxidant)
