# Insulin Sensitivity

## Definition
The body's responsiveness to insulin, affecting how efficiently cells take up glucose from the bloodstream. Higher sensitivity is generally associated with better metabolic health.

## Key Aspects
- Excessive body fat reduces muscle insulin sensitivity
- Reduced sensitivity worsens nutrient partitioning in hypercaloric diet
- Hypercaloric diet should be stopped when surplus reduces insulin sensitivity
- When sensitivity drops, weight gain becomes primarily fat rather than muscle
- Important consideration in muscle mass diet timing and duration
- Some supplements may improve sensitivity, but utility in healthy subjects is controversial
