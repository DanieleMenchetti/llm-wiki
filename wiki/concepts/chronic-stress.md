# Chronic Stress

## Definition
Prolonged stress that creates a generalized inflammatory state in the body, affecting multiple physiological systems.

## Key Aspects
- Activates HPA axis (hypothalamus-pituitary-adrenal)
- Increases cortisol release (stress hormone)
- Creates systemic inflammatory state
- Effects include:
  - Increased pro-inflammatory cytokines
  - Cellular aging and damage
  - Adipocyte hypertrophy (fat cell enlargement)
  - Metabolic syndrome (altered metabolism, abdominal fat, hypertension, insulin resistance)
  - Gut dysbiosis with possible mood alterations
  - Increased psychological stress and anxiety (gut-brain axis)
- Can cause nutrient deficiencies (Vitamin D, B3, folate, B6, B12, omega-3)
- Nutrition can be either cause or solution
