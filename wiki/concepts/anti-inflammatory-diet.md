# Anti-inflammatory Diet

## Definition
A dietary approach designed to reduce systemic inflammation in the body by avoiding pro-inflammatory foods and emphasizing anti-inflammatory nutrients.

## Key Aspects
- Avoids refined carbohydrates, fried foods, sugary drinks, red/processed meat, hydrogenated fats
- Avoids sugar (both refined and cane) due to high glycemic index
- Emphasizes tomatoes, olive oil, leafy greens, cabbage, nuts, fatty fish, berries, legumes
- Reduces risk of inflammation-related conditions
- Can help manage stress-related inflammation
