# Mediterranean Diet

## Definition
A dietary pattern characterized by high consumption of vegetables, legumes, whole grains, fish, and olive oil, with moderate consumption of other foods. Considered a model for overall health and brain health.

## Key Aspects
- Varied and balanced approach to nutrition
- Rich in vegetables, legumes, whole grains, fish, and olive oil
- Confirmed as winning model for brain health
- Helps maintain brain young and reactive longer
- Pillar: olive oil ("The Mediterranean ends where olives no longer grow")
- Protects blood vessels and improves cerebral circulation
- Should be followed with consistency for best results
