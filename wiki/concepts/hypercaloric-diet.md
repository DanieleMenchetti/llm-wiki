# Hypercaloric Diet

## Definition
A diet with caloric intake above maintenance needs, typically used to support muscle growth (hypertrophy) when combined with resistance training.

## Key Aspects
- Typically +10% above normal caloric needs, or +100-500 kcal surplus
- Surplus should come primarily from carbohydrates
- Lower fat intake (both percentage and absolute terms)
- Protein intake: 1.6-1.8 g/kg, not exceeding 2.2 g/kg
- Duration is relative — stop when fat gain becomes excessive
- Should only be undertaken if body fat ≤12-14% (for men)
- Larger surpluses (500 kcal) have shorter duration than smaller surpluses (100 kcal)
- Meal timing important, especially post-workout
