# Brain Nutrition

## Definition
The specific nutritional requirements of the brain to maintain optimal cognitive function, memory, concentration, and overall brain health.

## Key Aspects
- Brain consumes about 20% of total body energy
- Requires specific nutrients: good fatty acids, antioxidants, B vitamins, minerals, complex sugars
- Poor diet (saturated fats, refined sugars) triggers inflammation and oxidative stress
- Damages nerve cells and impairs cognitive function
- Key brain foods:
  - Fatty fish (omega-3 for cell membranes and nerve transmission)
  - Nuts and seeds (vitamin E, magnesium for neuroprotection)
  - Colorful produce (flavonoids, carotenoids for neuron protection)
  - Whole grains and legumes (complex carbs for steady energy)
  - Dark chocolate (flavonoids, endorphins for mood and concentration)
  - EVO oil (protects blood vessels, improves cerebral circulation)
- Lifestyle factors also critical: hydration, sleep, exercise
