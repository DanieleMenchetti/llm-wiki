# Cortisol

## Definition
A steroid hormone produced by the adrenal glands, often called the "stress hormone." Released in response to stress and affects various body functions.

## Key Aspects
- Released during stress response via HPA axis activation
- Called "hormone of stress"
- Chronic elevation causes abdominal fat accumulation
- Contributes to metabolic syndrome when chronically elevated
- Higher cortisol associated with higher BMI and obesity tendency
- Can cause insulin resistance
- Affects nutrient partitioning in hypercaloric conditions
- Dietary choices can influence cortisol levels
