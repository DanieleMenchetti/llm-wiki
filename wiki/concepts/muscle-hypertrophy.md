# Muscle Hypertrophy

## Definition
Increase in muscle cross-sectional area (size), as opposed to hyperplasia (increase in cell number). The primary goal of muscle mass diets combined with resistance training.

## Key Aspects
- Requires combination of resistance training and hypercaloric diet
- Training must include weight lifting with varied exercises and intensity
- Adequate rest is essential — protein synthesis and growth occur during recovery
- Hypercaloric diet provides energy and nutrients for growth process
- Controlled caloric surplus needed — not excessive to avoid fat gain
- Adequate protein intake essential to support construction
- Consistency and discipline required — results take time
- Impossible to grow only lean mass — some fat gain is inevitable
