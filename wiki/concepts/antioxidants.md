# Antioxidants

## Definition
 Compounds that protect cells from damage caused by free radicals (unstable molecules that can cause oxidative stress). Include vitamins, minerals, and plant compounds.

## Key Aspects
- Fight free radicals and prevent oxidative stress
- Protect nerve cells from aging and damage
- Slow neuron wear in the brain
- Found in colorful fruits and vegetables (flavonoids, carotenoids)
- Found in nuts and seeds (vitamin E)
- Found in dark chocolate (flavonoids)
- Essential for brain health and cognitive function
- Support overall cellular health
