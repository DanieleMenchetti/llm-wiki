# Omega-3 Fatty Acids

## Definition
Essential polyunsaturated fatty acids that the body cannot produce and must obtain from diet. Critical for brain function, cell membrane structure, and reducing inflammation.

## Key Aspects
- Fundamental for building brain cell membranes and nerve signal transmission
- Called "food for memory" — helps brain cells communicate better
- Found in fatty fish: salmon, tuna, mackerel, herring, sardines
- Deficiency contributes to stress and inflammation
- Recommended: include fatty fish at least twice weekly
- Also found in nuts and seeds (walnuts, flax seeds, chia seeds)
