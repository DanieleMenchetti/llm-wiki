# Dieta antistress

**Source**: [raw/Dieta antistress.md](../raw/Dieta%20antistress.md)
**Date**: 2022-01-12
**Type**: Article
**Author**: La Nutrizione.it

## Key Takeaways
- Chronic stress activates HPA axis, increases cortisol, causes systemic inflammation
- Stress effects include metabolic syndrome, gut dysbiosis, cellular damage, anxiety
- Nutrient deficiencies in stressed people: Vitamin D, B3, folate, B6, B12, omega-3
- Foods to avoid: refined carbs, fried foods, sugary drinks, red/processed meat, hydrogenated fats, sugar
- Bananas should be limited in case of anxiety due to tyramine content
- Recommended foods: tomatoes, olive oil, leafy greens, cabbage, nuts, fatty fish, berries, legumes
- Regular exercise helps reduce psychological stress

## Summary

This article explains how chronic stress creates a pro-inflammatory state in the body through activation of the HPA axis (hypothalamus-pituitary-adrenal) and increased cortisol release. This systemic inflammation leads to multiple negative effects: increased pro-inflammatory cytokines, cellular aging and damage, adipocyte hypertrophy, metabolic syndrome (altered lipid and glucose metabolism, abdominal fat accumulation, hypertension, insulin resistance), gut dysbiosis with mood alterations, and increased psychological stress and anxiety.

Poor nutrition during stress can lead to specific vitamin deficiencies (D, B3, folate, B6, B12) and omega-3 essential fatty acid deficiencies, which further contribute to organic stress. However, balanced dietary choices can reduce inflammation risk.

The article recommends avoiding pro-inflammatory foods: refined carbohydrates (white bread, sweets), fried foods, sugary drinks, red and processed meat, and hydrogenated/saturated fats like margarine and lard. Sugar (both refined and cane) should be avoided due to its high glycemic index and pro-inflammatory effects. Bananas should be limited in cases of anxiety, colitis, or nervousness due to tyramine content that stimulates the nervous system.

The anti-stress and anti-inflammatory diet includes tomatoes, olive oil, leafy green vegetables (spinach), cabbage, nuts (almonds, walnuts), fatty fish (salmon, mackerel, tuna, sardines), berries (strawberries, blueberries, cherries, oranges), and legumes. For legumes, the article recommends consuming decorticated or pureed legumes, or soaking them for 8-12 hours with apple cider vinegar to deactivate phytates.

Lifestyle factors are also important: regular exercise can positively contribute to reducing psychological stress.
