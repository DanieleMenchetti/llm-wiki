# Best Diet for Brain Performance

---

# The Mediterranean Diet

## Optimal for Brain Health

The Mediterranean Diet is confirmed as the winning model for brain performance.

**Key characteristics:**
- Varied and balanced approach
- Rich in vegetables, legumes, whole grains, fish, and olive oil
- Helps maintain brain young and reactive longer
- Should be followed consistently for best results

---

# Brain Energy Requirements

## The Brain is Energy-Hungry

- **Consumes 20% of total body energy**
- Requires specific nutrients:
  - Omega-3 fatty acids
  - Antioxidants
  - B vitamins
  - Minerals
  - Complex carbohydrates

Poor diet (saturated fats, refined sugars) triggers inflammation and oxidative stress, damaging nerve cells.

---

# Top Brain Foods

## 1. Fatty Fish

**Salmon, tuna, mackerel, herring, sardines**

- Rich in omega-3 fatty acids
- Fundamental for brain cell membranes
- Essential for nerve signal transmission
- Called "food for memory"
- **Recommendation:** Eat at least twice weekly

---

# Top Brain Foods

## 2. Nuts and Seeds

**Walnuts, almonds, flax seeds, chia seeds**

- Provide vitamin E and magnesium
- Protect nerve cells from aging
- Improve brain plasticity
- "Forever young" for the brain

---

# Top Brain Foods

## 3. Colorful Fruits & Vegetables

**Blueberries, strawberries, spinach, cabbage, carrots**

- Contain flavonoids and carotenoids
- Powerful antioxidants
- Fight free radicals
- Slow neuron wear

---

# Top Brain Foods

## 4. Whole Grains & Legumes

**Whole bread/pasta, rice, oats, quinoa, buckwheat, legumes**

- Provide complex carbohydrates
- Release energy slowly and gradually
- Maintain stable blood sugar levels
- Prevent concentration drops

---

# Top Brain Foods

## 5. Dark Chocolate

**70%+ cocoa**

- Contains flavonoids
- Stimulates endorphin production
- Improves concentration and mood
- Rule: "Little, but good"

---

# Top Brain Foods

## 6. EVO Oil

**Extra Virgin Olive Oil**

- Pillar of Mediterranean diet
- Protects blood vessels
- Improves cerebral circulation
- Facilitates oxygen and nutrient delivery to nerve cells

---

# Foods to Limit

## Excess Salt

- Can trigger inflammatory brain processes
- Directly raises blood pressure
- Use little salt — food already contains necessary sapidity

---

# Lifestyle Factors

## Diet Alone Is Not Enough

**Hydration**
- Drinking abundant water maintains efficient nerve impulse transmission

**Regular Sleep**
- Essential for brain function

**Exercise**
- Daily walk stimulates production of new brain connections (neuroplasticity)

---

# Summary

## The Complete Approach

**Diet:** Mediterranean pattern with brain-boosting foods
**Hydration:** Abundant water intake
**Sleep:** Regular, quality sleep
**Exercise:** Daily physical activity

Combined, these factors optimally support brain function and help maintain the brain young and reactive longer.

---

# Key Takeaways

1. **Mediterranean Diet** is optimal for brain performance
2. **Fatty fish** (2x/week) provides essential omega-3s
3. **Colorful produce** supplies antioxidants
4. **Complex carbs** provide steady energy
5. **Lifestyle matters:** hydration, sleep, exercise
6. **Consistency** is key for long-term benefits
