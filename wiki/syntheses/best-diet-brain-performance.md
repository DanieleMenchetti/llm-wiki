# Best Diet for Brain Performance

## Thesis
The Mediterranean Diet is the optimal dietary pattern for brain performance, providing essential nutrients that support cognitive function, memory, and concentration while protecting against inflammation and oxidative stress.

## Key Points

- **Mediterranean Diet** is confirmed as the winning model for brain health
- Brain consumes 20% of total body energy and requires specific nutrients
- Key nutrients: omega-3 fatty acids, antioxidants, B vitamins, minerals, complex carbohydrates
- Poor diet (saturated fats, refined sugars) triggers inflammation and oxidative stress, damaging nerve cells

## Supporting Evidence

From [[dieta-per-la-mente]]:
- Fatty fish (omega-3) is "food for memory" — fundamental for brain cell membranes and nerve signal transmission
- Nuts and seeds provide vitamin E and magnesium, protecting nerve cells from aging
- Colorful fruits and vegetables contain flavonoids and carotenoids that slow neuron wear
- Whole grains and legumes provide complex carbs for steady energy, preventing concentration drops
- Dark chocolate stimulates endorphin production, improving concentration and mood
- EVO oil protects blood vessels and improves cerebral circulation
- Excess salt can trigger inflammatory brain processes

From [[brain-nutrition]]:
- Lifestyle factors are critical: hydration, sleep, and exercise
- Drinking water maintains efficient nerve impulse transmission
- Regular sleep and daily walk stimulate production of new brain connections

From [[omega-3-fatty-acids]]:
- Omega-3s are critical for brain cell structure and function
- Deficiency contributes to stress and inflammation
- Recommended: include fatty fish at least twice weekly

From [[complex-carbohydrates]]:
- Complex carbs release energy slowly and gradually
- Maintain stable blood sugar levels, preventing concentration drops

From [[mediterranean-diet]]:
- Rich in vegetables, legumes, whole grains, fish, and olive oil
- Helps maintain brain young and reactive longer
- Should be followed with consistency for best results

## Contradictions
No contradictions found between sources. All sources consistently support the Mediterranean diet and similar nutritional principles for brain health.
